# Clinical Governance & SLP Advisory Guidelines

## 1. Practice Scope & Non-Diagnostic Boundary
**MANDATORY CLINICAL DISCLAIMER**:
> "BoloSpeech is an assistive home practice and telepractice monitoring tool designed to support structured speech sound practice under the guidance of a licensed Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP). BoloSpeech is NOT a diagnostic medical device, does not provide medical diagnoses, and does not replace professional clinical evaluation or personalized therapy from a licensed speech therapist."

## 2. Red-Flag Trigger Criteria
The platform continuously monitors longitudinal practice telemetry for clinical alert triggers:
1. **Sudden Regression**: A drop of >25% in accuracy across previously mastered phonemes over 2 consecutive weeks $\to$ In-app prompt to schedule an SLP consultation (possible neurological, hearing, or emotional etiology).
2. **Persistent Hypernasality or Breathiness**: Acoustic indicators flagged as potential velopharyngeal dysfunction or vocal cord pathology.
3. **Severe Fluency Escalation**: Rapid increase in secondary behaviors (head jerks, eye blinks) or struggle during disfluent episodes.

## 3. Developmental Norms Reference Table (Smit et al. / Dodd / Indic Pediatric Norms)

| Phoneme | Target Sounds | Typical Age of Acquisition (50% Mastery) | Age of Full Mastery (90% Mastery) | Clinical Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| /m/, /n/, /p/, /b/, /d/, /w/ | Bilabials & Early Stops | 2;0 - 3;0 | 3;6 | Early developing sounds; establish confidence |
| /t/, /k/, /g/, /f/, /h/ | Alveolars, Velars | 3;0 - 3;6 | 4;0 | Watch for velar fronting (/k/ $\to$ /t/) |
| /s/, /z/, /l/, /v/ | Fricatives & Liquids | 4;0 - 5;0 | 6;0 | Interdental lisp normal until ~4.5; lateral lisp is never developmental |
| /r/, /ɹ/ | Retroflex / Rhotic | 5;0 - 6;0 | 7;0 - 8;0 | High acoustic complexity; F3 lowering required |
| /ʈ/, /ɖ/ | Indic Retroflex Stops | 3;6 - 4;6 | 5;6 | Normal in Indic languages; expected variant in Indian English |
| /tʃ/, /dʒ/, /θ/, /ð/ | Affricates & Interdentals | 4;6 - 5;6 | 7;0 | Late mastered consonants across English & Spanish |
