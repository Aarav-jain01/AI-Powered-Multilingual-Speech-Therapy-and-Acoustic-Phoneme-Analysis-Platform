# Architectural & Engineering Decision Records (ADRs)

| ADR ID | Title | Status | Date | Decision & Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ADR-001** | Unified Phoneme Representation (IPA + Pan-Language Map) | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | All languages (English, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Spanish) map to an International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) representation with articulatory feature vectors (place, manner, voicing, aspiration, retroflexion). This allows acoustic scoring models to share feature spaces and prevents fragmentation across script systems. |
| **ADR-002** | Client-Side Acoustic Signal Analysis + Server ML Fallback | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | In-browser Web Audio API captures 16 kHz uncompressed PCM and extracts real-time features (pitch contour via YIN, formants via LPC/spectral peaks, zero-crossing rate, spectral centroid, VAD). This achieves <150ms real-time visual feedback (waveform, pitch, tongue placement) and guarantees offline capability with queued sync, while sending lightweight acoustic frames or WAV buffers to the backend inference service for forced alignment and GOP validation. |
| **ADR-003** | Code-Switching & Dialect Variant Acceptance Policy | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | Multilingual households frequently interchange lexical items (e.g., Hindi "pani" vs. English "water") and legitimate regional phonetic variants (e.g., Indian English /v/-/w/ merger, retroflex /ʈ/ /ɖ/ substitution for alveolar stops). Scoring is parameterized with an *Accepted Variants Register* per child and language set. Legitimate dialectal variations are marked as "acceptable variant" rather than an articulation error, preventing false rejections that harm children's confidence. |
| **ADR-004** | Non-Punitive Child UX Architecture | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | Children aged 3–12 never receive raw percentage scores or red "incorrect" markers. Feedback is framed through mastery progress, playful visual cues (star animations, playful sound waves, companion mascot "Boloo the Parrot"), and positive encouragement ("Great try! Let's curl our tongue back a tiny bit more"). Raw GOP scores and error classifications are strictly reserved for Parent and Clinician views. |
| **ADR-005** | Disfluency (Stammering) vs. Articulation Pipeline Separation | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | Disfluent speech (blocks, sound prolongations, syllable repetitions) must never be penalized by articulation scoring. A dedicated disfluency detection pipeline runs in parallel, calculating Percent Syllables Stuttered (%SS) as an informative metric for the SLP, while the child practice mode activates a pacing metronome and "easy-onset / light-contact" visual guidance. |
| **ADR-006** | Verifiable Parental Consent & Privacy Purge (DPDP 2023 / COPPA / GDPR-K) | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | Child voice is biometric-adjacent data. Consent requires verifiable adult verification. Audio retention is strictly opt-in: default retention is 30 days unless pinned by an SLP for clinical audit. A cryptographic deletion routine allows parents to purge all raw audio and derived acoustic feature matrices in compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023 and COPPA. |
| **ADR-007** | Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) Adaptive Engine | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | Practice stimuli are selected dynamically using a clinical hierarchy: Sound in Isolation -> Syllable (CV, CVC) -> Word -> Phrase -> Sentence -> Conversation. Transition probabilities are calculated using BKT parameters ($P(L_0)$, $P(T)$, $P(S)$, $P(G)$) calibrated against pediatric norms. |
| **ADR-008** | Clinician Human-in-the-Loop Override & Gold-Label Data Flywheel | Accepted | 2026-10-23 | Every AI assessment includes an uncertainty confidence value. Recordings with confidence <0.75 are flagged for SLP review. When an SLP corrects a phoneme segmentation or error tag, the clip is committed to the Gold-Label Evaluation Queue, directly generating retraining pairs for the CTC acoustic head. |
| **ADR-009** | Ink Orbit Architectural Grid & 3D Acoustic Resonance Hero | Accepted | 2026-10-27 | Adopted an architectural hairline-grid monochrome design system supporting Paper Light (`#FAFAFA`) and Obsidian Dark (`#09090B`) themes, paired with `Fraunces` italic editorial serif, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, and `JetBrains Mono` (`tabular-nums`). The hero embeds a draggable 7-part Three.js PBR liquid-ink acoustic sculpture (`RoomEnvironment` PMREM, `onBeforeCompile` harmonic surface displacement, WebGL2 `fenceSync` pacing on software GL, and graceful SVG fallback) coupled to the live code-switching sandbox. |
